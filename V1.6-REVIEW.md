# Dhanvin Pharmaceuticals — V1.6 Review

## Business Positioning
The homepage presents Dhanvin Pharmaceuticals as an India-based pharmaceutical and healthcare supply company established in 2024. It leads with institutional supply expertise, states the approved Government of Andhra Pradesh supply wording, and separates current business from future international ambitions.

## Our Expertise
- Pharmaceuticals
- Surgical Supplies
- Dental Equipment & Materials
- Laboratory Chemicals & Reagents
- Diagnostic Kits & Equipment

## Current Business
A prominent dark institutional section states: “Dhanvin Pharmaceuticals currently supplies quality medicines and surgical products to the Government of Andhra Pradesh.” It contains no additional institution, contract, department, tender or certification claims.

## Future Vision
The future section describes international healthcare partnerships and import/export as opportunities the company aims to explore. It does not suggest that international operations are already underway.

## Functional Testing
- Verified hero actions, navigation, mobile menu open/close, expertise anchors and category enquiry preselection.
- Confirmed phone, email and WhatsApp links use the supplied contact details.
- Confirmed empty required enquiry fields are rejected.
- Privacy and Terms routes both returned HTTP 200; cross-page navigation targets the homepage.
- No browser page errors were observed.

## Build
`npm run build` succeeded under Node.js 22.19 and generated the homepage, privacy and terms routes. Astro 7.3.8 is installed; `npm install` previously reported zero vulnerabilities.

## Spelling Check
The former misspelling, “Phar&#97;maceticals”, was searched project-wide and returned zero raw matches. The displayed company name is “Dhanvin Pharmaceuticals”.

## Remaining Limitations
- The enquiry form uses `mailto:` and requires the visitor to send the prepared message from an email application; there is no backend or database.
- There is no CMS, ecommerce, ordering, inventory or payment functionality.
- Privacy and website terms information still needs review for production use.
- Canonical metadata and sitemap setup await the final website domain.
- Node.js 22.19 or newer is required by the current Astro dependency tree.
